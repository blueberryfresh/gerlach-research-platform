# Gerlach Personality Research Platform - User Guide

**Version 1.0 | February 2026**

---

## Welcome!

Thank you for participating in our research study on human-AI collaboration and personality types. This guide will help you navigate the research platform and complete your session successfully.

---

## 📋 What You'll Do

Your participation involves:

1. **Complete a personality assessment** (10-15 minutes)
2. **Review your randomly assigned task** (2-3 minutes)
3. **Collaborate with AI to solve a task** (20-30 minutes)
4. **Provide task-specific responses** (5-10 minutes)
5. **Complete a post-experiment survey** (10-15 minutes)

**Total Time**: 45-60 minutes

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Registration (2-3 minutes)

**What you'll see:**
- Welcome screen with study information
- Participant ID input field
- Consent checkbox

**What to do:**
1. Read the study information carefully
2. Enter your Participant ID (provided by researcher)
   - Example: `P001`, `P042`, `PARTICIPANT_123`
3. Check the consent box: "I consent to participate in this research study"
4. Click **"Begin Research Session"**

**Tips:**
- Use the exact Participant ID given to you
- Make sure you understand the study purpose before consenting

---

### Step 2: Big Five Personality Assessment (10-15 minutes)

**What you'll see:**
- 50 personality questions organized by trait
- 5-point rating scale for each question

**What to do:**
1. Read each statement carefully
2. Rate how much you agree or disagree:
   - 1 = Strongly Disagree
   - 2 = Disagree
   - 3 = Neutral
   - 4 = Agree
   - 5 = Strongly Agree
3. Answer all 50 questions
4. Click **"Submit Assessment"**

**Tips:**
- ✅ Answer honestly - there are no "right" or "wrong" answers
- ✅ Choose what best describes you, not how you wish to be
- ✅ If unsure, select "Neutral" (3)
- ✅ Don't overthink - go with your first instinct
- ✅ Take your time - this is important for the research

**What happens next:**
- Your personality scores will be calculated
- You'll proceed to your assigned task

---

### Step 3: Task Introduction (2-3 minutes)

**What you'll see:**
- Your randomly assigned task description
- Instructions for the task

**What to do:**
1. Read the task description carefully
2. Make sure you understand what you are being asked to solve
3. Click **"Start Task Dialogue"** when ready

**Tips:**
- Read slowly and thoroughly before starting
- The task and AI are assigned automatically — there is nothing to select

---

### Step 4: Task Dialogue (20-30 minutes)

**What you'll see:**
- Task document displayed on screen
- Chat interface with your assigned AI
- Message input box at the bottom

**What to do:**
1. **Read the task document** carefully
2. **Start chatting** with the AI about the task
3. **Collaborate** to complete the task together
4. **Exchange 20-40 messages** (typical range)
5. Click **"End Dialogue"** when you're satisfied

**How to Collaborate:**

**DO:**
- ✅ Engage naturally, like talking to a colleague
- ✅ Share your genuine thoughts and opinions
- ✅ Ask questions if you're unsure
- ✅ Disagree with the AI if you think differently
- ✅ Take time to think through your responses
- ✅ Build on each other's ideas
- ✅ Express uncertainty when appropriate

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Rush through the conversation
- ❌ Simply agree with everything the AI says
- ❌ Treat it like a test with "right answers"
- ❌ Worry about grammar or perfect phrasing
- ❌ Pretend to understand if confused

**Example Dialogue Flow:**

```
⭐ AI: Hello! I'm excited to help you with this task. 
Shall we start by reviewing the requirements?

👤 You: Yes, that sounds good. I think the main goal 
is to evaluate the candidates based on their experience 
and cultural fit.

⭐ AI: Great observation! Let's create a systematic 
approach. I suggest we evaluate each candidate on three 
criteria: leadership, technical skills, and cultural fit. 
Does that work for you?

👤 You: That makes sense, but I think we should also 
consider their potential for growth, not just current 
skills.

⭐ AI: Excellent point! You're right - growth potential 
is important. Let's add that as a fourth criterion...

[Conversation continues...]
```

**Tips:**
- Quality over quantity - thoughtful responses matter more than speed
- It's okay to change your mind during the conversation
- The AI is there to help, not to test you
- Take breaks if needed (but don't close the browser)

---

### Step 5: Task-Specific Response (5-10 minutes)

After ending the dialogue, you'll complete a task-specific form.

#### If You Are Assigned Noble Industries:

**What you'll see:**
- Ranking form for 5 candidates (A, B, C, D, E)
- Text boxes for rationale

**What to do:**
1. **Rank each candidate** (1-5, where 1 = top choice)
   - Each rank must be unique
   - Use all ranks (1, 2, 3, 4, 5)
2. **Provide rationale** for each ranking
   - Write 2-3 sentences explaining WHY
   - Be specific about your reasoning
3. Click **"Submit Rankings"**

**Example:**
```
Candidate A: Rank 1
Rationale: "Candidate A has the strongest leadership 
experience with 10 years in manufacturing. Their track 
record of process improvements aligns perfectly with 
the role requirements."
```

#### If You Are Assigned Popcorn Brain:

**What you'll see:**
- 4 slider scales (1-7) for self-assessment

**What to do:**
1. **Rate yourself** on 4 creativity dimensions:
   - **Originality**: "I created unique ideas to solve the problem"
   - **Flexibility**: "I presented alternative ideas"
   - **Elaboration**: "I created details to add to ideas"
   - **Fluency**: "I created many ideas to solve the problem"
2. Use the sliders (1 = Strongly Disagree, 7 = Strongly Agree)
3. Click **"Submit Assessment"**

---

### Step 6: Post-Experiment Survey (10-15 minutes)

**What you'll see:**
- 33 questions about your experience
- Mix of rating scales and open-ended questions

**Question Types:**

**Part 1: Rating Questions**
- Rate statements on a 1-7 scale
- 1 = Strongly Disagree
- 7 = Strongly Agree
- 4 = Neutral

**Example questions:**
- "I am confident that our work is done correctly"
- "I enjoyed the LLM-Human collaboration"
- "The LLM showed compassion towards me"
- "I felt the LLM understood my perspective"

**Part 2: Open-Ended Questions**
- Write detailed text responses
- Share your thoughts and experiences

**Example questions:**
- "Describe any positive impacts from the LLM"
- "Discuss the compatibility of you and the LLM"
- "How did the LLM's personality play a role?"

**What to do:**
1. Read each question carefully
2. Provide honest, thoughtful responses
3. Be specific with examples when possible
4. Write as much or as little as you want for text questions
5. Click **"Submit Survey"** when complete

**Tips:**
- ✅ Be honest - your feedback is valuable
- ✅ It's okay to be critical
- ✅ Provide specific examples from your experience
- ✅ Take your time - quality feedback helps the research

---

### Step 6: Session Complete

**What you'll see:**
- Completion message confirming your session is done

**What happens next:**
- Your session is complete!
- You can close the browser
- The researcher will contact you about next steps (if applicable)

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Problem: Page won't load

**Solutions:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try a different browser (Chrome recommended)
3. Clear browser cache: `Ctrl+Shift+Delete`
4. Try incognito/private mode
5. Contact researcher for support

### Problem: Can't submit a form

**Solutions:**
1. Make sure all required fields are filled
2. Check for error messages (in red)
3. Scroll up to see if you missed any questions
4. Try clicking submit again
5. Refresh the page (your answers should be saved)

### Problem: Lost my place / Browser closed

**Solutions:**
1. Return to the same URL
2. Enter your same Participant ID
3. The system should resume where you left off
4. If not, contact the researcher

### Problem: AI is not responding

**Solutions:**
1. Wait 10-15 seconds (responses may take time)
2. Check your internet connection
3. Refresh the page
4. If problem persists, contact researcher

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use my phone or tablet?**  
A: Not recommended. Use a computer or laptop for the best experience.

**Q: Can I take breaks?**  
A: Yes, but don't close the browser. Your progress is saved.

**Q: What if I disagree with the AI?**  
A: That's perfectly fine! Express your disagreement - it's valuable data.

**Q: How long should my dialogue be?**  
A: Typically 20-40 messages. Focus on quality, not quantity.

**Q: Can I change my task or AI?**
A: No. Both are randomly assigned by the system and cannot be changed.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: Don't worry! Just continue naturally. There are no "wrong" answers.

**Q: Is my data anonymous?**  
A: Yes. Only your Participant ID is used, not your personal information.
