🎯 Participant Workflow

Gerlach Personality Research Platform

Complete Study Journey: 45-75 minutes

1
ACCESS & REGISTRATION
⏱️ 2-3 minutes

What Participant Receives:

  • Study URL: https://gerlach-research.streamlit.app/ (English) / https://blueberryfresh-research-ko.streamlit.app/ (Korean)
  • Unique Participant ID (e.g., P001, KOREA_042)
  • USER_GUIDE.docx (optional)

What Participant Does:

  1. Opens URL in web browser
  2. Enters Participant ID
  3. Reads and provides informed consent ✓

✅ Session Created → Moves to Big5 Assessment

↓
2
BIG5 PERSONALITY ASSESSMENT
⏱️ 10-15 minutes

IPIP-50 Questionnaire:

  • 50 questions total (10 per trait)
  • 5-point Likert scale:
    • 1 = Strongly Disagree
    • 2 = Disagree
    • 3 = Neutral
    • 4 = Agree
    • 5 = Strongly Agree

Example Questions:

• "I am the life of the party"
• "I sympathize with others' feelings"
• "I am always prepared"
• "I get stressed out easily"
• "I have a vivid imagination"

System Calculates:

  • Big5 scores (0-100 per trait)
  • Gerlach personality type: Average, Role Model, Self-Centred, or Reserved
  • Confidence score

✅ Personality Profile Complete

↓

6
POST-EXPERIMENT SURVEY
⏱️ 8-12 minutes

37-Question Survey:

A. LIKERT SCALE QUESTIONS (31 questions, 1-7 scale):

  • LLM personality perception
  • Collaboration experience
  • Task difficulty
  • Satisfaction with outcome
  • Trust in LLM suggestions

B. OPEN-ENDED QUESTIONS (6 questions):

  • "What did you like most about the LLM personality?"
  • "What challenges did you face?"
  • "How did the LLM influence your decisions?"
  • "Would you use this LLM again? Why/why not?"
  • "Any suggestions for improvement?"
  • "Additional comments"

✅ Survey Responses Saved

↓
